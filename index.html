Remembering a Titan of Industry

Ratan Tata

Industrialist | Philanthropist | Chairman, Tata Group

An Indian industrialist and philanthropist whose quiet leadership transformed the Tata Group into a global conglomerate while never losing sight of the people his decisions were meant to serve.

About

A Builder Who Believed in People

Ratan Naval Tata was born on 28 December 1937 in Bombay to Naval Tata, who had been adopted into the Tata family. He studied architecture and structural engineering at Cornell University before returning to India to work his way up through the Tata Group from the shop floor.

As chairman of Tata Sons from 1991 to 2012, he turned a largely domestic conglomerate into an international business spanning steel, automobiles, technology and hospitality, while remaining known throughout for his humility, restraint and deep sense of responsibility toward the country he served.

Life Journey

From Cornell to the Chairman's Office

1937

Born in Bombay

Ratan Naval Tata was born on 28 December 1937 to Naval Tata and Sooni Tata, into a family already central to Indian industry.

1962

Joining the Tata Group

After completing his studies at Cornell University, he joined Tata Industries and later worked on the shop floor of Tata Steel, learning the business from the ground up.

1991

Becoming Chairman

He succeeded J.R.D. Tata as chairman of Tata Sons, taking charge at a moment when India's economy was opening up to the world.

2000s

Building a Global Group

Under his leadership the Tata Group acquired Tetley, Corus and Jaguar Land Rover, and in 2009 launched the Tata Nano, envisioned as an affordable car for Indian families.

2012

Stepping Down as Chairman

After more than two decades at the helm, he retired as chairman in December 2012 and was named Chairman Emeritus of Tata Sons.

2024

A Life Remembered

Ratan Tata passed away on 9 October 2024 in Mumbai, leaving behind a legacy of principled leadership and generous philanthropy.
